# Hoarfrost: Snow & Blizzards

Cosy, realistic snowy weather: seven kinds of snowfall and blizzard, flakes carried by a living wind, soft snow settling on the land and a quiet winter soundscape.

## Features

- Seven kinds of snowy weather: light snow (silent), snow, heavy snow, heavy snow with large flakes, light blizzard,
  blizzard, heavy blizzard.
- A living wind with gusts and lulls that flows over and around the land; flakes carried by it.
- Soft, smooth snow settling on the land: drifts in the lee, even cover under trees, caps on posts, a little snow blown
  into cave mouths.
- Wind charges, explosions, gusts and feet blow up powder that drifts and settles.
- Footprints, real drifts on the server, bright snowy sky and haze, a quiet winter soundscape.
- Optional [Serene Seasons](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/serene-seasons) integration.

## Requirements

[NeoForge](https://neoforged.net) 1.21.1. Optional: [Serene Seasons](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/serene-seasons).
