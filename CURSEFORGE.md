# Hoarfrost: Snow & Blizzards

**Cosy, realistic snowy weather** - seven kinds of snowfall and blizzard, flakes carried by a living wind, soft snow
settling on the land and a quiet winter soundscape.

![A blizzard sweeping through a snowy spruce forest](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Pocky-l/hoarfrost/main/docs/screenshots/blizzard.jpg)

📦 **Requires** [NeoForge](https://neoforged.net) 1.21.1 or [Forge](https://files.minecraftforge.net) 1.20.1 · works on the client alone, on the server too for shared
snow and real drifts

## ✨ Features

- ❄️ **Seven kinds of snow** - light snow, snow, heavy snow, large flakes, light blizzard, blizzard and a whiteout heavy
  blizzard, each with its own flakes, wind, haze and sound, gliding into each other. In snowy land it snows under a
  clear sky too, with a clear spell now and then.
- 🌬️ **A living wind** - turns, gusts and lulls, rises over hills and walls, is calm behind them and barely reaches
  under roofs; flakes, crystals, clumps and grains drift with it.
- 🏔️ **Soft snow on the ground** - one smooth cover on land, roofs and crowns, with drifts in the lee, wind-swept ridges
  and caps on fence posts; out to the render distance, melting near lamps.
- 💨 **Snow with physics** - wind charges, explosions, gusts and running feet blow up powder; footprints that depend on
  the ground (a rim of snow on soil, crisp prints on stone, slips that sweep the ice bare) and trodden paths that fresh
  snow fills again.
- 🌫️ **Storms with volume** - clouds of snow rolling over the land, a bright overcast sky and a blue-grey haze, never
  indoors or underground.
- 🌲 **Snow on the trees** - green leaves with a cap of snow on the crowns that falls off when you brush, bump or break
  them, and real snow drifts piling up when the mod is on the server.
- 🔇 **A quiet winter soundscape** - the hush of falling snow, wind growing to a howl, muffled indoors, footsteps
  crunching and squeaking.

[![Sodium](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Pocky-l/hoarfrost/main/docs/compat/sodium.png)](https://modrinth.com/mod/sodium) ✅ **[Sodium](https://modrinth.com/mod/sodium)** - works, snowy leaves included.

[![Living Wind](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Pocky-l/hoarfrost/main/docs/compat/living-wind.png)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/living-wind) ✅ **[Living Wind](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/living-wind)** (optional) - the snow flies and drifts the way its wind vanes point, and a soft snowfall or a blizzard blows harder or softer with its wind.

## ⚙️ Configuration

*Mods > Hoarfrost > Config*: a quality preset and a switch for every effect. Per world: chances of each kind of snow,
seasons and drifts. Operators can force the weather with `/hoarfrost weather <kind>`.
